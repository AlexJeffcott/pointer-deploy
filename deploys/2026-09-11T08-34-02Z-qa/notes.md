draws it and nothing is fetched for it. `/` reads "No unit is placed here yet. The frame drew this
view, and nothing was fetched for it," and the `@browser` scenario counts the requests the page
takes after the frame is on screen rather than believing that sentence.

recorder's own verification traffic - and the first that removed a unit. `--drop hello` is why
`hello` left: removal is said, never inferred from what the tree happens to build. Inferring it is
what wrote the pointer the previous image refused on 2026-09-10, and a cold machine answered 503
for a whole region on that pointer. TODO §36 carries the readings.
